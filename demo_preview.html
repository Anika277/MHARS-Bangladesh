Plain-Language Emergency Survival Protocols Public Safety
Flood Safety Directives
  • ✅ DO: Move drinking water, dry food, and torchlight to high ground.
  • ✅ DO: Switch off electricity at the main breaker before leaving.
  • ⛔ DON'T: Never walk or drive through moving floodwater (15cm knocks you down).
  • ⛔ DON'T: Do not touch fallen power lines or wet sockets.
Earthquake Safety Directives
  • ✅ DO: Drop, Cover & Hold On under a sturdy table.
  • ✅ DO: Move to open ground away from power lines after shaking.
  • ⛔ DON'T: Never use elevators during or right after tremors.
  • ⛔ DON'T: Do not stand near glass windows or heavy shelves.
